Ours (original work)

GRAVELIGHT ONE's stand-in C-47 was made for GRAVELIGHT by the project itself: it is generated by
tools/c47/build_c47.py and tools/c47/c47_airframe.py from primitives and published dimensions only, with no
third-party mesh, texture or scan (the markings atlas is drawn by tools/c47/make_decals.py with the bundled OFL
fonts). It may be used, changed and shipped with the game like the rest of the project's own code.
