Ours: original work made for GRAVELIGHT (T-091).

Every file under assets/ours/characters/ is generated by the project's own scripts
(tools_src/blender/characters/*.py: make_body.py, make_kits.py, make_textures.py, build_all.py) from the project's
own data (data/gravelight/art/characters.json, the ZombieRig export tools_src/blender/characters/rig.json). No
third-party mesh, texture, scan or photograph is used. Owned by the GRAVELIGHT project; same terms as the game.
