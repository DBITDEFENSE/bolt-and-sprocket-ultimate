Ours: original work made for GRAVELIGHT (T-092).

Every file in assets/ours/characters/people/ is generated by the project's own scripts
(tools_src/blender/characters/build_people.py with make_body.py, make_face.py, make_kits.py) from the project's own
data (data/gravelight/art/people.json, characters.json, the ZombieRig export rig.json). No third-party mesh, texture,
scan or photograph is used; no likeness of a real person (the Cousins use the Likeness Sheet's written defaults only).
