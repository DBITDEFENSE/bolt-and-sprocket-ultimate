## 1. THE STORY: "THE MARK"
The zombies come from a chip, a mark, not only a sickness. Nachtglas (DARK-GLASS™) is the raw material; Projekt Wiederkehr cuts it into tiny violet glass chips, "Marken", implanted under the skin of the right hand or the forehead, sold as a loyalty and identity tag ("the mark that keeps you"): Darkmatter's subscription token in the flesh. When a marked person dies the mark keeps the body running ("nobody cancels"). The relay signal (the Ledger on Earth; the relay on the Moon) keeps marks active, which is why cutting the relay in the Fernflug finale stops the dead. Deacon names it ("the mark in the right hand or the forehead…"). Peg's orders were to retrieve marks. Wren keeps finding them on the dead. Jakub refused the mark. Respect rule: never a camp tattoo; forced laborers were not marked. Gameplay: every zombie shows a faint violet mark (hand or forehead) pulsing with the infection shader; hitting it is a weak point (×1.5, small Scrip bonus for a mark kill). Update CANON, LORE, TIMELINE, intel and Archive; VO lines that say sickness/virus (log in STORY_CHANGES); ~20 new lines; 5 new intel documents (an implant order, a recruiting poster, a medic's notes, Darkmatter "terms of the mark", Jakub's refusal).
