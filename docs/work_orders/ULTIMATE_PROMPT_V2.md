## 0. EXTRA RULES FOR THIS MAP

1. **Alternate history, told with respect.** This is a pulp-horror "what if." The real V-2 program was built by concentration-camp forced labor, and thousands died building it. That history is treated with dignity:
   - The forced laborers are **never zombies, never enemies, and never spectacle.**
   - In this map they are the heroes: their real-world sabotage of the rockets is how the player wins (§4).
   - Never recreate real atrocity imagery for shock value.
2. **No real Nazi symbols.** No swastikas, no SS runes, no real unit insignia. Use a fictional, consistent faction emblem for "the Reich's" Projekt Wiederkehr, defined in `docs/ART_FACTIONS.md`: a stylized black eagle-less sunburst over a split bell. This keeps the game sellable everywhere and keeps the focus on our story.
3. **Civilians in the launch cutscene** are shown through skylines, shelters, sirens, and the sky itself. There is no on-screen civilian gore.
4. **Rocket and propellant details stay at the level of what a museum placard would say:** "liquid oxygen and alcohol," "guidance gyros," "fuel valves." No real engineering specifics. This is a game, not a manual.

## 1. STORY — "OPERATION MORGENROT" (write the full version in `docs/MAP3_MORGENROT.md` and update `TIMELINE.md`)

**When / where:** April 1945, alternate history. A hollowed peak in the Bavarian Alps called **the Hohe Wacht**, above the fictional village of **Sankt Aurel**. The war is lost and everyone knows it. Projekt Wiederkehr has one desperate card left.

**The plan (Operation Morgenrot, "Dawn Red"):** At dawn, a **salvo of Nachtglas-tipped V-2 rockets** will launch from the Hohe Wacht. Each warhead carries the reanimation agent refined from the heart fragment Reinholt escaped with at the end of Kaltenbrunn. The warheads air-burst high over **London, Antwerp, and Paris** and rain violet Nachtglas fire and ash over the cities. Everyone the ash touches who dies will rise. The goal is to turn the Allies' cities against them and force a negotiated peace.
- **Range:** an ordinary V-2 could never reach London from the Alps. These are **"V-2N"** rockets, boosted by Nachtglas resonance (Darkmatter's Next-Era propulsion, though nobody here knows that name).
- **The lead rocket:** the **SONNENSPEER ("Sun-Spear")**, a giant Nachtglas-boosted prototype standing in the crater pad. It carries Reinholt's fragment itself, and its guidance computer synchronizes the whole salvo. **Kill the Sonnenspeer's launch and the salvo goes with it.**

**Why each faction wants it:**
- **The Reich command** wants a miracle weapon.
- **Dr. Augusta Reinholt** wants something else. The "Woman in Violet" (Sable Vantage, unnamed here; see v1 §10) promised her **Liesl back** once "distribution reaches scale." For Reinholt, the rockets aren't a weapon. They're the payment.
- **Darkmatter** wants its test market "to go national." The Operator calls the launch **"the regional rollout."** Gravelight characters still never learn what that means (v1 tone rules).
- **Dr. Emil Fasbender** is held at the summit observatory, forced to compute the salvo's trajectories. His doubtful loyalty pays off here: he is genuinely trying to help the squad, hiding sabotage hints inside his calculations. He may not survive the night (the endings decide).
- **The forced laborers** who built the rockets in the mountain's tunnels left hidden **sabotage marks**: chalk symbols showing the welds they weakened, the gyros they miscalibrated, and the valves they left loose. Most of them are gone. Their marks remain. **Wren's brother appears on a transfer list from Kaltenbrunn to the Hohe Wacht**, and one of the chalk marks is his. This closes Wren's arc.

**Character payoffs (each needs a scene or VO beat):**
- **Wren** finds her brother's mark and finally closes her notebook.
- **Peg's sealed orders** (retrieve Nachtglas at any cost) come to a head at the end (§5).
- **Deacon** talks to the Sonnenspeer like a frightened horse while he sabotages it.
- **Dutch** carries the team up the mountain and complains the entire time.
- **Bolt and Vendelin**, if picked (v1 §11.9), each get a full quote pack. Vendelin recognizes Darkmatter propulsion in the rocket and is horrified. Bolt and Sprocket recognize the Moon-ending crack (§6).

## 3. NEW CONTENT ON THIS MAP

**Enemies** (data-driven; full details in `docs/ENEMIES.md`). All of them use the §7 animation upgrade.
- **Gängers (Morgenrot outfits):** rocket troops, white-coat technicians, cable-car staff, mountain troops. **No laborers. Ever.**
- **Treibstoff-Gänger** (round 8+): launch crew in rubberized fuel suits, dripping alcohol. **Killing one with fire or explosives makes it burst into a burning puddle** (hurts everyone, including zombies: use it).
- **Frostbiss** (round 12+, near the depot): LOX-burned, frost-crusted, slow, hard-hitting. Their hits frost-slow you. They shatter like Glasshides.
- **Bergwacht** (round 15+): mountain troops with crampons and ice axes who **climb cliff faces and walls** and drop in from above. This is a new spawn behaviour, so build the climb into the AI (§7.3).
- **Special round: "LUFTALARM"** (every 5–7 rounds, replaces Bellringers on this map). Sirens wind up, searchlights sweep, and **Fallers** plunge out of the night sky: burnt test-launch casualties, glowing hot, that hit the ground in a shockwave. Avoid the searchlight-marked impact circles. Clearing the round guarantees a Resupply.
- **BOSS — DER STARTMEISTER** (round 14–16, then periodically): the launch officer, fused into a pressure suit and a towed fuel bowser. Phases: sprays burning alcohol in arcs; drags the bowser to ram you; when the bowser is breached he becomes a running fire-trail. The weak point is the valve-wheel on his back. He drops the **Launch Key (Schlüssel I)** on a specific Easter-egg step.
- **FINAL BOSS — REINHOLT, "WIEDERKEHR"** (Easter-egg only, §4.2 step 9).

**Wonder weapon — "DIE LANGE NACHT" (The Long Night)**, a buildable cryo-lance. Its 3 parts are a LOX flask (Fuel Depot), a nozzle (Assembly Hall), and a pressure regulator (Dam). Build it at the Leitstand workbench. It sprays a cone of liquid-oxygen fog that freezes zombies solid, and frozen zombies **shatter** to melee or bullets. Upgraded at the Crucible: **"Polarnacht"** (longer cone, freeze spreads between frozen targets). Lore: the rocket's own fuel, turned against it.

**Crate additions:** the **Panzerfaust** (tube launcher), the **Gewehr 1-Lance**, a fictional **Fallschirm-pattern automatic rifle**, and the Siren Gramophone (with a new record). Give Crucible names in the v1 style (e.g. Panzerfaust → "Late Postage").

## 4. THE LAUNCH CLOCK & THE MAIN EASTER EGG — "LETZTES LICHT" (Last Light)

### 4.1 The Launch Clock
- It **starts when power is turned on**: the crews wake, the gantry lights come up, and the Zählwerk boards start flipping.
- Countdown is measured in **rounds**: base **T-minus 25 rounds** after power (tunable). Shown on the Leitstand boards, in the Operator's announcements ("Regional rollout in twelve cycles. Thank you for your patience."), and in the dawn sky.
- **Holds:** each sabotage step (§4.2) adds a **+2-round HOLD** ("scrub" lines over the PA, cursing technicians on the radio).
- **Fueling surges:** leaving the Kessel unattended for 2+ full rounds after power advances the clock by 1 (the crews finish their work). This pressures players to come back.
- **Pacing setting:** "Story Pacing: Standard / Relaxed." Relaxed starts the clock only when Easter-egg step 7 begins, so solo and casual players aren't rushed.
- **Clock hits zero without the Easter egg completed → the LAUNCH ENDING (§5.2) plays.** It is not a game over: the match continues in the "Aftermath" state (§5.2).

### 4.2 Steps
Solo- and co-op-doable. A data-driven step graph with per-match checkpoints and dev-console skips (v1 rules). No hidden hard-fail steps except the clock itself, which is the point.
1. **Power on** at the Dam. The clock starts. The Operator's first line on this map: *"Valued customers. The regional rollout begins at dawn."*
2. **Reach the Sternwarte** by Bergbahn. Fasbender, through the bars of his cell: the salvo is slaved to the Sonnenspeer's guidance, and "the people who built these rockets left you a map. Look for the chalk."
3. **The Witnesses' Marks.** Find **6 chalk sabotage marks** (randomized from 12 spots) across the Stollen, Meiller Lanes, and Depot. Each glows faintly when you're near and plays a single whispered line (a name and a hometown). Near each one, the faint silhouette of a laborer appears briefly at work, then fades. They are memorial figures, never interactive, never hostile. Wren's brother's mark is always one of the six, and finding it triggers her scene.
4. **Sabotage the salvo** (any order; each one gives a +2 HOLD). Each Meiller rocket has a different method, revealed by its mark:
   - **LONDON:** open 4 fuel valves in the order the chalk shows while zombies pour in; a wrong valve vents frost on you.
   - **ANTWERPEN:** carry a miscalibrated gyroscope from the Assembly Hall to the rocket. While carrying it you can't sprint or shoot (one-handed pistol only); teammates escort.
   - **PARIS:** freeze the warhead coupling with Die Lange Nacht, then shatter it with melee.
   - Each sabotaged rocket **topples off its trailer** in a big physics moment and kills zombies in the fall.
5. **Build Die Lange Nacht** (can be done earlier; needed for PARIS and step 9).
6. **The First Key.** Kill Der Startmeister **inside the Kessel while the gantry floodlights are on** (switched on at the Leitstand, which draws extra spawns). He drops **Schlüssel I**.
7. **The Second Key.** Fasbender's cell can only be opened from the Himmelsrechner: solve a relay puzzle (light 5 relays in the order of the music-box melody **from Kaltenbrunn**, re-randomized per match and replayed on Liesl's shrine music box). Fasbender gives you **Schlüssel II** and the abort sequence. Choose here whether to take Fasbender with you (he follows, can be downed, and gives hints) or leave him at the summit (safer; changes his ending line).
8. **Heartstorm at the summit.** Reinholt hijacks every speaker and stops the clock herself: *"No more delays."* She triggers a **5-minute real-time final countdown** (separate from the round clock). LUFTALARM Fallers, Bergwacht, and Gängers come in waves.
9. **REINHOLT — "WIEDERKEHR."** She has dosed herself with her fragment and floats in the Kessel inside a violet storm. A spectral, broken **Liesl apparition** (made of Nachtglas light, with the tin drummer marching at her feet) shields her.
   - **Phase 1:** freeze the shield with Die Lange Nacht and shatter it.
   - **Phase 2:** she summons the dead of Kaltenbrunn (callbacks to Map 1 enemies, the Foreman included).
   - **Phase 3:** she clings to the gantry and the Sonnenspeer's engines begin to light.
   - Her dialogue is grief, not villainy.
10. **ABORT.** A two-key turn in the Leitstand (Schlüssel I + II). In co-op, two players must turn their keys within 1 second of each other. In solo, Fasbender turns the second key if you brought him; otherwise you jam the second key with a demolition charge and have 10 seconds to turn yours. At the same time, the fuel umbilicals on the gantry must be cut (3 points, Die Lange Nacht or melee). **Success → the ABORT ENDING (§5.1).** If the 5-minute countdown runs out first → the LAUNCH ENDING (§5.2).

**Rewards** (main Easter egg): all perks for the rest of the match (above the cap); a permanent 5th perk slot on Morgenrot; the final **Character Dossier pages** for all four squad members (the full secrets resolve here); the **Gravelight saga credits**; a big XP bonus; the "Letztes Licht" calling card; an Archive entry. If the Moon egg (§6) was done, the Moon ending replaces the Abort ending.

## 7. ZOMBIE ANIMATION & DESIGN UPGRADE (game-wide; applies retroactively to every Gravelight map and Helvetia Works)

Goal: zombies that feel heavy, individual, and reactive, a clear step up from stock "zombie walk" loops, without breaking the 24-active-zombie performance target. Write `docs/ZOMBIE_ANIM_SPEC.md` first.

### 7.1 Rig & pipeline
- One shared humanoid skeleton for all zombie types, using Godot 4's retargeting (BoneMap / SkeletonProfileHumanoid). That way every animation source (Mixamo, CC0 packs, our own) retargets onto every body. Record each animation's source and license in `ASSETS.md`.
- **AnimationTree** per zombie, with a state machine (Spawn / Emerge / Climb / Barricade / Locomotion / Attack / React / Crawl / Death) and blend spaces inside the states. **Root motion** drives movement where possible, and nav speed matches the stride, so there is **no foot-sliding**.

### 7.2 Locomotion
- **Variants per speed tier**, picked randomly per zombie: shamble ×3, walk ×3, jog ×2, run ×3, sprint ×2, crawler ×2 (belly-drag and four-limb scuttle).
- **Per-instance variation:** playback-speed jitter of ±10%, a random start phase (no synchronized hordes), and a **posture trait** applied as an additive layer: limp (left/right), hunch, dislocated hanging arm, broken neck tilt, or dragged foot.
- Turn-in-place animations; lean into turns at run and sprint speed.
- **Foot IK** on stairs, slopes, and rubble (raycast foot placement with a hip offset). **Head look-at** toward the nearest player (LookAtModifier3D or equivalent, limited cone, smoothed).

### 7.3 Interactions
- **Barricades:** 3 tear-board variants, a reach-through swipe at players near the window, 2 vault/climb-in variants, and a drop-down for high windows.
- **Climbing** (new, needed for Bergwacht): a wall/cliff climb loop with ledge mantle and drop-in, driven by authored climb volumes on the navmesh (NavigationLink3D).
- **Attacks:** 4 swipe variants, a runner lunge, a sprinter grab that briefly pulls the player's view, a crawler bite, and armless zombies switching to headbutt/bite only.

### 7.4 Hit reactions & physics
- **Directional additive flinches** by hit zone (head / torso / left-right arm / left-right leg). **Stagger** at damage thresholds. **Knockdown + get-up** (front and back variants) from explosives, traps, and the shatter-shockwave.
- **Partial ragdoll on flinch:** the arms and head blend into physics briefly (PhysicalBoneSimulator3D or equivalent) so hits look like hits.
- **Deaths:** 8+ authored deaths (headshot snap, gut collapse, spin, knee-drop, backward fall…) that blend into full ragdoll after the key pose. Explosive deaths launch the ragdoll. Caps: max 6 simultaneous ragdolls; corpses settle, then fade or sink (Nachtglas "dissolve" shader) after 8–12s.

### 7.5 Dismemberment
- Separable pieces: forearms, upper arms, lower legs, head (pop on headshot kill), and jaw. Each has **stump caps** and wound decals. Severed pieces are physics props on a short lifetime.
- **Gameplay hooks:** losing an arm changes the attack set; losing a leg mid-run triggers a **fall-into-crawler** transition animation; a headless body staggers 1–2 steps before dropping.

### 7.6 Visual design
- **Modular build:** base bodies (several builds/heights, male and female) + an **outfit kit per map/faction** (Kaltenbrunn miners and lab staff, Crossroads villagers and soldiers, Basel dockworkers, Morgenrot rocket troops / technicians / fuel crews / mountain troops) + gear pieces + random tint, dirt, and frost.
- **Nachtglas infection shader:**
  - violet veins under the skin whose emission **pulses in sync with the map's low hum**;
  - glowing eyes with bloom;
  - crystal cracks that **grow with round number**, so late-round zombies look more crystallized. This is a visual difficulty tell.
- **Damage-state materials:** wound decals at hit points, burn char, frost crust, LOX-frozen, and soaked-in-fuel sheen (Treibstoff-Gänger).
- **Silhouette discipline:** every special enemy must read from 40 m in fog, by shape AND by audio tell.
- **Gore setting:** Off / Low / Full (Off hides dismemberment and blood, keeping the shatter VFX).

### 7.7 Audio sync & performance
- Animation events drive footsteps (surface-aware), board rips, attack whooshes, and vocal breaths/snarls per zombie type, so sound matches motion.
- **Animation LOD:** full rate within 15 m, half rate at 15–35 m, quarter rate beyond, no IK or look-at beyond 25 m, and impostor-light distant crowds if needed. Profile with 24 active zombies on Kaltenbrunn and Morgenrot; the 60 FPS target still holds.
- **Acceptance test:** a side-by-side capture (before/after) of 10 zombies walking, tearing a barricade, getting shot in each limb, and dying. Post it in the milestone summary.
