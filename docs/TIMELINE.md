# Master timeline

Keep this file in step with `CANON.md`. Dates on Earth are exact where the story depends on them. B&S-era dates are relative, because that galaxy is "the far future" and never gives an Earth year. **(UPDATE 11, T-190)** The rows marked *Lazarus* are the origin layer (`CANON.md` §0, `docs/LAZARUS.md`); what is real history and what is ours is tabled in `docs/LAZARUS.md` §11.
