# Master timeline

Keep this file in step with `CANON.md`. Dates on Earth are exact where the story depends on them. B&S-era dates are relative, because that galaxy is "the far future" and never gives an Earth year.

## Earth

| When | What | Where it's told |
|---|---|---|
| **9 Nov 1938** | Darkmatter test-market probe **TM-SOL3-001** comes through a fold and strikes the Kaltenbrunn salt mine. The miners call it a meteorite. Field Merchandiser Unit **V-1** survives the crash, damaged and out of contact. | Fine Print Ch. 2 |
| Nov–Dec 1938 | V-1 repairs himself from probe salvage. The **first retained miner** rises near the substrate. V-1 builds a disguise and learns to pass among the miners. | Ch. 2 |
| Early 1939 | V-1 walks out of the mine in a borrowed coat. Behind him in the dark, the Darkmatter Customer Retention Interface (the future **Operator**) boots for the first time. | Ch. 2 ending |
| 1939 | **Liesl Reinholt** dies of diphtheria. The Reich's mining authority seizes the "meteorite" find. | Gravelight LORE |
| **1940** | "Herr Viktor Vendelin, Ingenieur" founds **Helvetia Automaten AG** in Basel and meets delivery driver **Res Gähwiler**. | Ch. 3 |
| 1941 | First Reich contract for tonic dispensers. **Projekt Wiederkehr** begins under Dr. Augusta Reinholt. Ironhide Stout (Otto Kessler) and Twin Barrel Bitters (Major Anselm Brück) are made. | Ch. 3 |
| 1941–42 | Second Wind Schnapps (Sister Agnieszka; the hidden battery), Trigger Tonic (Dr. Ilse Vogt), Marathon Malt (Res's grandmother's recipe), Packmule Porter (Feldwebel Gustl Ammer). | Ch. 3 |
| 1942 | Hawkeye Brandy, derived from tests on Halina "Sowa" Kurek. **Vendelin starts sabotaging.** "The Woman in Violet" (Sable Vantage) offers Reinholt Liesl back, as a service. | Ch. 3; Reinholt's recordings |
| **1943** | Blastproof Bock. Res smuggles a sample through his cousin Anni to the SOE, and British intelligence learns Nachtglas exists. Vendelin's first message to Dr. Fasbender. | Ch. 3 |
| 1943–44 | Vendelin designs the unreleased prototypes: Fine Print Fernet, which Darkmatter refuses to sell, and an unfinished Overclock Cordial blueprint. | Ch. 3 |

| **23 Dec 1944** | Gravelight One is brought down beside a crossroads inn. **Map 0: Crossroads Inn**, that night. | Gravelight |
| Late Dec 1944 | **Map 1: Kaltenbrunn**. The main egg ends with the heart collapsing, Reinholt fleeing by rail with a fragment, and Peg pocketing a shard. The collapse opens a fold (**the Return Trip**). | Gravelight; CANON §9 |
| **17–18 Mar 1945** | **Helvetia Works** (Fine Print Ch. 5). The squad steps out of the fold into Basel. Bolt & Sprocket arrive via Blartz's misfired pad. The Underwriter comes to recall Vendelin under Clause 0. Vendelin's last transmission is sent at **03:12 on 18 March**. The finale: Vendelin overrides Clause 0 and cuts the fold, which returns its contents to sender. Bolt & Sprocket go home; the squad goes back to late December 1944. Vendelin stays in 1945. | Ch. 5 |
| Late Dec 1944 (again) | The squad is back where they started. They remember Basel as a fever dream, and the dates in Peg's notebook don't add up. | Ch. 5 epilogue; Gravelight Map 2 intro |
| Jan 1945 | **Map 2: Totenhafen** (outline). A Baltic U-boat pen shipping Nachtglas out. | Gravelight |
| Mar 1945 | **Map 3: Morgenrot** (outline). An Alpine observatory-fortress where Reinholt tries to wake Liesl. Her Darkmatter deal comes due, and the moral choice includes refusing the offer for her. "Season Two." | Gravelight |
| After Mar 1945 | Vendelin works to shut the substrate down from inside 1945. | Season Two hook |
