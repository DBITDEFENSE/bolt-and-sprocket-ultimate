# Maps 3 and 4 rebuilt: Totenhafen and Fernflug to the bar of Crossroads Inn and Kaltenbrunn (UPDATE 09 §5)

The lead's design direction (sections 1–3), then the audit worker's "as built" findings (section 4, filled in from
the `u09_before` captures), then the per-zone plan each zone worker executes (section 5). Captures: before in
`morning/screenshots/u09_before/`, after in `morning/screenshots/u09_after/` (git-ignored; counts and a contact
sheet are reported in `morning/MORNING_REPORT.md`).

## 1. The bar
Crossroads Inn and Kaltenbrunn read as **places**: a floor of worn planks or cobbles with a real material, walls
with a skirting, a cornice, trim and openings that are framed, furniture that tells who lived there, light with a
source you can see (a lamp, a stove, a window with weather behind it), fog, snow and dust in the air, sound that
changes room by room, people, paper, chalk, rust, and the war outside the windows. Maps 3 and 4, as generated by
`gen_map02.gd` and `gen_map04*.gd`, are the same primitives (`B`, `OB`, `CYL` boxes with one material key) at
plan-level: the layouts, zones, doors, spawns, loops and systems are right and must not move; the **surfaces,
silhouettes, props, lighting and sound** are what is missing. The rebuild changes the look, never the gameplay:
timings, hitboxes, navmesh walkable area, prompts and door costs stay (contract: "Gameplay timings, hitboxes,
navmesh and prompts don't change in an art pass"). Where a wall gains thickness or trim, the navmesh is re-baked and
the smokes prove the loops still run.

## 2. How to build it (every zone worker)
1. **Extend the generators, never hand-edit the `.tscn`.** `gen_map02.gd` / `gen_map04a-d.gd` + `gen_map04_lib.gd`
   stay the source of truth; the scene is regenerated headless (`"$GODOT" --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path .
   --script res://gravelight/tools_src/gen_map02.gd`, see each file's header) and committed. Read how gen_map00 and
   gen_map01 dress a room (`_dress_*` functions, `PropLibrary`, `MaterialLibrary`, decals, `_lumi_line`,
   `pendant`, `bulkhead`, `flood`) and bring the same vocabulary over.
2. **A shared detail library** (`gravelight/tools_src/gen_detail_lib.gd`, written by the first zone worker that needs
   it, extended by the others; keep functions pure and documented): corrugated iron sheets, I-beams and trusses,
   riveted plate strips, pipes with flanges and valves, cable trays, rails and sleepers, chain-link and railings,
   crane gantries, concrete with form-board lines (a material + a trim), window frames with mullions and broken
   panes, doors with frames and hardware, stairs with treads and stringers, catwalk grating, bollards and cleats,
   posters and notices (`paper_decor.gd`), chalk marks (Jakub's alphabet where the canon says so), puddles, rust
   streak decals, snow drifts, sand drifts, regolith.
3. **Free assets first** (CC0 / CC-BY, no login): Poly Haven models and textures (concrete, metal, wood, rust, salt
   flat, moon), ambientCG materials, Kenney packs (industrial, nature), Quaternius (crates, barrels, modular
   industrial), OpenGameArt props. File them under `assets/third_party/<source>/<id>/` with `SOURCE.txt`,
   `LICENSE.txt`, a credits row in `docs/ASSETS.md` and `python -I tools/assets_manifest.py` passing. Blender-scripted
   pieces (`tools/build_world_objects.sh` pattern) only for what has no good download: the flying wing's ribs, the
   U-boat hull, the Kranich wreck, the relay dish.
4. **Budgets (contract C4):** ≤ 2500 draw calls in view, ≤ 6 shadowed lights per zone, ≤ 400k triangles in view
   (≤ 200k on web); use `merge_static_meshes`, `BakedMultiMesh` for repeated props, `ShadowProxies` for the batches,
   and `WebProfile` must still get the mood (depth fog + emissive instead of volumetrics).
5. **Every zone gets:** a floor material and a wall material with trim; at least one hero silhouette; 3+ story props
   from `data/gravelight/story_props.json` / `props_story.gd`; lighting with visible sources and one key light; a
   sound zone (`zone_volume.gd` + `data/gravelight/audio.json` ambience per zone); ambient life (steam, drips, dust,
   gulls, a radio, a loose sheet of iron banging in the wind); the bank branch where §3 placed it; ammo boxes where
   MAPS.md puts them; minimap re-bake (`tools/bake_minimap.sh` through `tools/wsl_godot.sh` if it renders, else the
   headless form); navmesh re-bake with `check_mapdata.sh` clean.
6. **Acceptance per zone:** before/after captures (`tools/wsl_capture.sh u09_after only=<map>`; add camera sets per
   zone to `data/gravelight/capture_cams.json` and a capture target for map04a-d in `tools_src/capture.gd` if
   missing), a 10-minute headless soak (`tools/ai_soak.sh --map=<map> --bots=3 --minutes=10`) with no new navmesh
   snags in the stuck log, the map's smokes green (`smoke_map02`, `smoke_m2`, `smoke_map04a-d`, `smoke_map04_flow`,
   `smoke_map04_full`), the tide / act transitions proven by those smokes, and the draw-call census within budget.

## 3. Design direction per zone (the lead's calls)

### Totenhafen (`map02`): a Baltic U-boat pen in a January storm
Palette: wet grey concrete, black water with sodium and violet reflections, rust, zinc, the orange of a hurricane
lamp, snow blowing sideways through every opening. The bunker is **mass**: 3 m walls with form-board lines, blast
doors, drip stains; the town above is ruin and fish.
- **A Quay:** the cutter wreck is the hero (a real hull: clinker planks, a wheelhouse, a lashed wheel, nets and
  floats); the harbourmaster's hut gets a tarred roof, a stove pipe, a tide table on the wall; bollards, cleats,
  coiled rope, fish crates, oil drums, a gull or two on the parapet; the sea beyond the parapet moving (the existing
  water shader with whitecaps), a breakwater light blinking; the bunker face with the stencilled name, a blast door
  with its counterweight, drip lines. Sound: surf, wind, the halyard slapping the hut's pole.
- **B Cannery:** zinc tables with fish scales glinting, the conveyor with cans, brine tanks with lids, pallets, hooks on
  rails, the infirmary partition with cots, a screen, a stove, Agnieszka's letter on the cot (already). Lighting: one
  skylight row with snow on the glass, pendant lamps, the stove's glow.
- **C Workshop:** torpedoes on real cradles (a cylinder with fins, hatches, a propeller), the overhead gantry with a
  chain hoist, the tool wall, the lathe, the trolley track with a loaded trolley, the warhead store cage with the lead
  coffins (violet seam). Lighting: caged bulkhead lamps, the welding bench's flicker.
- **D Pen Hall:** the hero zone. Three wet docks with real water, U-1047's hull (a proper Type VII silhouette: saddle
  tanks, the tower with its railings and periscope housings, the deck gun, a gangway), piers with kerbs, bollards and
  pole lamps, the crane with its hook and cab, the coffins on pallets, catwalks with grating and handrails, the pen
  mouth's steel curtain with the storm beyond it (lightning), water drips from the ceiling, the sodium light
  reflecting in the docks. Sound: the pens' echo, dripping, the curtain's groan, the tide alarm.
- **E U-boat interior:** bulkheads with round hatches, pipes and valves everywhere, bunks, the torpedo room with the
  Crucible strapped in, red battle lighting, the hull creaking.
- **F Lower pens:** the pump deck: pumps with flywheels, pipes, grilles, the Pumpenschalter, water at tide height,
  bulkhead lamps, the drowned sailors' effects.
- **G Signal tower:** the teletype, a chart table, the signal lamp, windows over the storm, the tower swaying in gusts
  (a subtle camera shake zone), Hinnerk Petersen's spot (T-135).

### Fernflug Act II (`map04b`): der Kranich in flight (the act that is a box today)
The whole act is **inside a flying wing**, and it has to feel like one: curved ribs every 1.5 m, spars running the
span, riveted skin panels with visible stringers, crawlways through the wing roots, frames with lightening holes,
cable runs and control rods moving, placards. Outside every window, blister and breach: **the sky at night**, clouds
moving past below, the coast and then the ocean, the moon, searchlights and flak over London (the window event),
fighters strafing past (streaks + sound), turbulence (the existing push system + a camera shake + hanging things
swinging), wind noise rising at breaches.
- **A Bombenschacht:** bomb bay doors with their actuators, six racks of casings (chalk marks), catwalk grating, the
  ramp at the back, a breach where a casing fell out: the ocean below.
- **B Mittelrumpf:** galley, bunks with harnesses, the radio room with the Bordfunk deck, the Crucible strapped down,
  oxygen bottles, a map table.
- **C the wing crawlways** (whatever MAPS calls them): the ribs and spars as corridors, fuel cells, inspection
  lamps, the sky through hull breaches.
- **D Triebwerk:** the port nacelle: the impeller core behind lead glass (violet, turning), the engineer's station,
  heat shimmer, pipes, the boss arena's floor plates.
- **E Kanzel:** four perspex blisters with gun mounts, the London window looking back and down.
- **F Cockpit:** two seats, the autopilot cradle, instruments lit, the glass being the whole front with the night
  outside.
Build approach: the fuselage/wing shell is one Blender-scripted piece (ribs + skin as a lofted surface with a
rivet-strip texture), the rooms are dressed with the detail library. A `FlightSky` node (sky, clouds as a scrolling
layered quad set or a cheap volumetric cloud sheet, the coast/ocean as a scrolling textured plane far below, the
fighters as scripted strafes) replaces the current diorama.

### 4.6 Findings that apply to all five scenes (plan items)
1. **One noise texture on every wall/ceiling** (Map 2's concrete, Act I's brick, Act III's walls): no trim, no panel seams, no weathering decals. A material pass (distinct floor, wall, trim per zone) is the cheapest big win.
2. **No visible light fixtures** outside Map 2's pendants and pole lamps; the acts have shafts of light from nothing, or Kaltenbrunn's crystal chandeliers hanging in an open yard (04a B/E).
3. **No per-zone sound.** `data/gravelight/audio.json` `music.ambient` has beds only for `map00_crossroads` and `map01_kaltenbrunn`; map02 and the four acts fall back to `ambient_map00` (the inn's wind). There is no `ZoneAmbience` node (nothing crossfades a loop when `ZoneVolume` changes). Plan item for the first zone worker of each map: write `gravelight/world/zone_ambience.gd` (child of `ZoneVolume`: `bed` key + `gain_db` + `fade_s`, handled by `AudioDirector`), add beds `ambient_map02`, `ambient_map04a..d` (and per-zone loops) to `audio.json`, and source CC0 loops (see section 5.0).
4. **No outside**: Act II's windows, Map 2's quay sea, the pen mouth's storm are not visible; the sky is black. A `FlightSky` / pen-mouth storm card is a priority.
5. **No life** (steam, drips, gulls, a banging sheet of iron, radio) in any acts zone; Map 2 has smoke and a few fires only.
6. **Compatibility renderer vs Forward+**: depth fog and emissive must carry the mood for `WebProfile`; keep every new light cheap (<= 6 shadowed per zone).
7. The acts' generators are small (335-477 lines) and use only `Props`-light vocabulary; `gen_detail_lib.gd` (section 2.2) is not written yet, so the first worker of Act I writes it and the others extend it.

**Gameplay-neutral wall thickening (what may gain thickness without moving a loop).** Walls are generated by `wall_x/wall_z` at `T = 0.3`; the navmesh is baked from the geometry and the loops are paths between solids, so a wall may grow **away from the walkable side**: Map 2: the quay parapet (outward, to the sea), the bunker face `MassFace` (already 3 m, any trim goes on its south or north faces only if it stays within 0.15 m of the face; the pen passage and window closets keep their 3 m depth), cannery/workshop outer walls (outward), hull sides (outward only; the infirmary partition stays 0.3 and gets non-colliding trim). Act I: housing blocks, assembly-hall and hangar outer walls (outward), the Kraftzentrale annex (outward). Act II: the hull skin outside the deck rectangle; ribs/spars may be non-colliding dressing. Act III: hangar/motor-pool outer walls, the lab and silo walls (into the rock, away from the hall). Act IV: dome hulls (outward). **Never** thicken a wall that bounds a loop lane, a door frame (doors are `door(c, y, w)` openings: frames are dressing outside the opening), a window closet, a link point (`LINKS`), or a `NavBlock_*` box. All trim, skirting, cornices, door frames, pipes, rails and decals are `Dressing` (no collider). Rebake with the generator, run `tools/check_mapdata.sh`, then the smokes.
