# Weapon models: source, licence, parts, scale (generated)

**2026-11-12 (T-241 b):** the 13 OpenGameArt models were smooth-shaded and bevelled in place by `tools_src/blender/smooth_palette_guns.py` (part nodes unchanged); their triangle counts in `models.json` are the new ones, the tables below still show the pack's originals.

Generated by `python -I tools/intake_guns.py` (T-087, UPDATE_05A section 3) from its intake table and the Blender conversion reports; the same data is `data/gravelight/weapons/models.json`. Do not edit the tables by hand: change the table in `tools/intake_guns.py` (or the `ANIMS` dict once phase 2 retargets pack animations) and re-run with `--no-convert`.

**Frame.** Our rig (`WeaponModels`, `WeaponHolder`): barrel along -Z, +Y up, origin at the right-hand grip, metres. Every GLB below is as exported (glTF axes, +Y up, the pack's own origin = bounding-box centre): `forward` is the axis the muzzle points along, `scale` brings the model to its real length. Phase 2 (u05-guns) normalises: rotate about Y (+X -> +90 deg, -X -> -90 deg, -Z -> 0, +Z -> 180), scale, move the grip to the origin, then places Muzzle / SightPoint / FrontSight / LeftHand markers and runs `tools/ads_check.sh`.

## Viewmodel fit (T-089)

`gravelight/weapons/weapon_glb.gd` (`WeaponGLB`) builds each gun above from its GLB: normalised by the `native` block, the pack's meshes regrouped under our contract names at their pivots, markers (Muzzle, SightPoint, FrontSight, LeftHand) and metas (reload, length, port, bore, grip_r, lh_axis...) from a geometry scan and the procedural model of the same id, then hand-fit in `data/gravelight/weapons/model_fit.json` (its `_doc` lists every key). All surfaces go through the shared `assets/materials/shaders/weapon.gdshader` (`WeaponShader`): wood grain, edge wear, bluing, scratches, cavity grime, frost per map, Nachtglas veins for the Crucible version; the OGA palette guns get all their detail from it. `GLSettings.values["greybox_guns"]` (vm_shots `--greybox`) switches back to the procedural guns for A/B. Fit sheets: `gravelight/tools_src/glb_fit_shots.tscn`; numbers: `glb_fit_dump.tscn`; alignment: `tools/ads_check.sh` (the markers and, since T-182, the sight geometry round the aim point: `SightPicture`; `--autofit` prints a fit).

**Weapon art rules.** Downloaded only for real guns (never modelled); the barrel along -Z, origin at the grip, real length (+-10 %, tested); moving parts named for the holder; one weapon shader for every gun; the Crucible look is a material on the same mesh; reload / draw timings come from weapons.json, never from a model.

- `walther_p38`: Slide carries the rear notch; the front blade is fixed to the frame (the P38's open-top slide).
- `webley`: Barrels hinge at the frame front; Cylinder is parented under Barrels so the break tips both; Mk IV model for the game's Mk VI. T-182: the rear marker sits in the top strap's notch (it was 2 mm under it, so the frame hid the blade).

## Wonder weapons (T-090)

## Conversion notes

- OGA `.blend`: one mesh per gun under an armature whose bones (body, slide, bolt, mag, hammer, trigger...) are the vertex groups; `convert_to_glb.py --split-vgroups --map` turns the dominant group of each face into a separate named object (several groups can merge into one name), drops the armature, applies transforms and exports the 8x8 `PichuliruPalette.png` with nearest filtering (the glTF sampler keeps `magFilter` NEAREST). Rounds inside a magazine are merged into `Mag`; the en-bloc clip and its rounds into `Clip`; a revolver's rounds into `Cylinder`.
- Sketchfab GLB: Sketchfab normalises geometry to a [-1, 1] box and carries the scale on the root matrix; after the transform is applied the models are ~2 m long, so `scale` (real length / model length) is 0.43-0.56. Node names were meaningless (`Plane.003`, `c_low`); they were identified from Workbench previews (`morning/screenshots/u05/intake/`) and renamed. Textures: 4K maps capped at 2K, small parts 1K / 512 by texel density, black emissive maps dropped, everything re-encoded as PNG.
