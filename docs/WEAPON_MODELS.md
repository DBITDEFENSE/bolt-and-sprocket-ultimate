# Weapon models: source, licence, parts, scale (generated)

Generated by `python -I tools/intake_guns.py` (T-087, UPDATE_05A section 3) from its intake table and the Blender conversion reports; the same data is `data/gravelight/weapons/models.json`. Do not edit the tables by hand: change the table in `tools/intake_guns.py` (or the `ANIMS` dict once phase 2 retargets pack animations) and re-run with `--no-convert`.

**Frame.** Our rig (`WeaponModels`, `WeaponHolder`): barrel along -Z, +Y up, origin at the right-hand grip, metres. Every GLB below is as exported (glTF axes, +Y up, the pack's own origin = bounding-box centre): `forward` is the axis the muzzle points along, `scale` brings the model to its real length. Phase 2 (u05-guns) normalises: rotate about Y (+X -> +90 deg, -X -> -90 deg, -Z -> 0, +Z -> 180), scale, move the grip to the origin, then places Muzzle / SightPoint / FrontSight / LeftHand markers and runs `tools/ads_check.sh`.

**Priority.** Devin's Sketchfab downloads are the hero viewmodels (Thompson, M1 Garand, Kar98k); the OpenGameArt "Ultimate Low Poly Guns" set (TastyTony / Pichuliru, CC-BY 4.0) is the baseline for every other gun and the fallback / third-person LOD for the three heroes (`alt`). Guns with no model keep the procedural greybox.

## Conversion notes

- OGA `.blend`: one mesh per gun under an armature whose bones (body, slide, bolt, mag, hammer, trigger...) are the vertex groups; `convert_to_glb.py --split-vgroups --map` turns the dominant group of each face into a separate named object (several groups can merge into one name), drops the armature, applies transforms and exports the 8x8 `PichuliruPalette.png` with nearest filtering (the glTF sampler keeps `magFilter` NEAREST). Rounds inside a magazine are merged into `Mag`; the en-bloc clip and its rounds into `Clip`; a revolver's rounds into `Cylinder`.
- Sketchfab GLB: Sketchfab normalises geometry to a [-1, 1] box and carries the scale on the root matrix; after the transform is applied the models are ~2 m long, so `scale` (real length / model length) is 0.43-0.56. Node names were meaningless (`Plane.003`, `c_low`); they were identified from Workbench previews (`morning/screenshots/u05/intake/`) and renamed. Textures: 4K maps capped at 2K, small parts 1K / 512 by texel density, black emissive maps dropped, everything re-encoded as PNG.
- Size: see `docs/ASSETS.md` (generated block) for per-folder sizes.
